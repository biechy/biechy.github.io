Jailbreaks for Black-Box Uncertainty Quantification in Large Reasoning Models

RL alignment makes reasoning models overconfident, and the usual black-box tricks barely help. J4U borrows ideas from jailbreaks to loosen the model up and get much better calibrated uncertainty, even on a closed production model.

Lucas Biéchy, Cédric Eichler, Adrien Boiret, Nicolas Anciaux

Under reviewICLR 2027 · 2026

PrivacySkills: How Privacy Guidance Shapes Source Selection in LLM Agents

Even when a harmless option exists, agents dig into confidential sources in 30% of runs. A system prompt alone barely helps, but adding privacy labels to the skills themselves roughly halves it.

Lucas Biéchy, Christophe Rechdan, Cédric Eichler, Héber Arcolezi, Nicolas Anciaux

Under reviewAISTATS 2027 · 2026

Testing a Learner Changes It: Identifying Biased Self-Evaluation from Actions with Verifiable Probes

Agents that grade their own work can get stuck on a bad option, and watching their choices won't reveal it. We show an auditor can catch this with tasks whose answer it already knows, and give the optimal way to do it.

Lucas Biéchy, Yuxiao Li, Zhonghao He, Tianyi Qiu

WorkshopBiAlign Workshop @ NeurIPS 2026 · 2026

From Observation to Intervention: Identifiability of Latent User Bias in Human-AI Interaction

By just watching, a biased user looks exactly like an unbiased one, so no amount of data helps. We prove that a short sequence of well-chosen interventions is enough to tell them apart.

Lucas Biéchy, Yuxiao Li, Zhonghao He, Tianyi Qiu
