I like research that goes all the way: from a theorem, to an experiment, to the people it ends up affecting.

These days that means privacy in LLM agents, and figuring out what models really do when all we get to see is their outputs. I want AI that makes people more capable and more free, and I think getting there takes both solid maths and good tools for the people using it.

Jailbreaks for Black-Box Uncertainty Quantification in Large Reasoning Models

RL alignment makes reasoning models overconfident, and the usual black-box tricks barely help. J4U borrows ideas from jailbreaks to loosen the model up and get much better calibrated uncertainty, even on a closed production model.

Lucas Biéchy, Cédric Eichler, Adrien Boiret, Nicolas Anciaux

Under reviewICLR 2027 · 2026

PrivacySkills: How Privacy Guidance Shapes Source Selection in LLM Agents

Even when a harmless option exists, agents dig into confidential sources in 30% of runs. A system prompt alone barely helps, but adding privacy labels to the skills themselves roughly halves it.

Lucas Biéchy, Christophe Rechdan, Cédric Eichler, Héber Arcolezi, Nicolas Anciaux

Under reviewAISTATS 2027 · 2026

Testing a Learner Changes It: Identifying Biased Self-Evaluation from Actions with Verifiable Probes

Agents that grade their own work can get stuck on a bad option, and watching their choices won't reveal it. We show an auditor can catch this with tasks whose answer it already knows, and give the optimal way to do it.

Lucas Biéchy, Yuxiao Li, Zhonghao He, Tianyi Qiu

02 · What I work on

Three threads

Privacy in LLM agentsMain focus

What do agents do with our personal data?

Where agents go looking for information, what they do with it, and which kind of privacy guidance actually changes that. Most of my upcoming work is here.

  • PrivacySkillsICLR 2027

Black-box uncertainty

How sure is the model, really?

Getting calibrated uncertainty out of reasoning models when you have no access to logits, only to what they answer.

  • J4UICLR 2027

Behavioural evaluations

What can behaviour alone tell us?

A biased agent or a biased user can look perfectly normal from the outside. I work out when watching isn't enough, and which tests give them away.

  • Testing a Learner Changes ItAISTATS 2027
  • From Observation to InterventionBiAlign Workshop @ NeurIPS 2026
