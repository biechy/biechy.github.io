01Statistical Modeling

Statistical modeling is one of the many branches of mathematics whose aim is to represent phenomena, in this case random ones. This branch is divided into two sub-categories: parametric modeling and non-parametric modeling. Each of these adopts a distinct approach, which leads us, in this journey, to approach each problem in these two forms. In any case, it is essential to know this chapter to better understand machine learning.

04Optimization

Optimization is a theoretical methodology framework to find the minimum of a function. When you know that, you can understand that is really important in ML where your work is to find extrema, particularly the risk ones. Optimization is a field in its own right.
